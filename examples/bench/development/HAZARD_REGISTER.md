# Preliminary hazard register

All entries are open. This is a seed for qualified review, not an accepted safety analysis.

| ID | Hazard | Initial severity | Release-closing evidence |
|---|---|---:|---|
| HAZ-001 | Uncontained rotor or blade debris | Catastrophic | Rotor integrity, containment analysis/test, and test-cell containment approval |
| HAZ-002 | Uncontrolled fire or fuel release | Catastrophic | Fire-zone design, fail-closed isolation, drainage, detection/suppression, and test |
| HAZ-003 | Inability to shut down | Catastrophic | Independent overspeed trip, fuel isolation, remote E-stop, and fault-injection test |
| HAZ-004 | Overspeed or overtemperature | Critical | Control limits, independent protection, sensor accuracy, and transient testing |
| HAZ-005 | Bearing or lubrication failure | Critical | Rotordynamics, bearing-life, oil-system rig, debris monitoring, and abort thresholds |
| HAZ-006 | Engine mount or test-stand failure | Catastrophic | Limit/ultimate loads, proof test, conformity, and inspection |
| HAZ-007 | Hot exhaust, noise, or blast injury | Critical | Exclusion-zone and facility engineering approval |
| HAZ-008 | Instrumentation disagreement masks unsafe state | Critical | Redundancy, calibration, voting/abort policy, and fault-injection test |

Any unexplained rub, vibration, leak, fire, overspeed, overtemperature,
instrumentation disagreement, or containment concern closes the active test gate.
