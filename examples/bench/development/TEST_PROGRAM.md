# Contained ground-test program

No instruction here authorizes physical testing. The qualified test director's
approved procedures, facility limits, permits, and emergency plan control execution.

## Evidence sequence

1. Material coupons, dimensional/NDT methods, blade/root specimens, casing proof,
   rotor spin, bearing/lubrication rig, and injector/combustor rig.
2. Manufacturing conformity: serialized critical parts, material/process records,
   FAI, NDT, rotor-stack balance, calibrated tools, and build book.
3. Facility readiness: engineered containment, exclusion zone, remote operation,
   fuel containment/isolation, fire detection/suppression, ventilation, exhaust,
   noise controls, calibrated acquisition, abort logic, and drills.
4. Progressive operation: dry motoring, oil-system validation, ignition checks,
   first light-off, stable idle, shutdown, acceleration/deceleration, derated points,
   thermal stabilization, and controlled envelope expansion.
5. Envelope/endurance: demonstrate every approved operating point and transient,
   complete the partner-approved endurance schedule, then full teardown,
   dimensional inspection, NDT, deterioration assessment, and anomaly closure.

`DEMONSTRATOR_VALIDATED` requires signed endurance/teardown acceptance and a final
ground-demonstrator report. It never means airworthy, flight approved, or certified.
