# Required partner work packages before Design Freeze R1

1. Cycle and operability: design/off-design cycle, inlet loss, mass flow, compressor
   maps and surge margin, turbine work balance, combustor loss, nozzle matching,
   and proposed ratings/limits.
2. Aerodynamics: hub-to-tip airfoils, stage loading, secondary flows, tip leakage,
   diffuser, combustor pattern factor/cooling, turbine cooling, and exhaust flow.
3. Structures and lifing: proof/burst, LCF/HCF, creep, fracture, attachment roots,
   casing and mount loads, blade containment, and declared inspection/life limits.
4. Rotating system: critical speeds, mode shapes, forced response, unbalance,
   bearing loads/life, preload/end float, seals, oil jets, sumps and scavenge.
5. Controls and instrumentation: start/idle/acceleration logic, fuel scheduling,
   overspeed/overtemperature protection, flameout response, shutdown independence,
   sensor ranges/accuracy/transients, fault detection, and test-cell interlocks.
6. Tolerance and thermal growth: cold/hot stacks, rotor orbit, casing distortion,
   tip/rim/axial clearances, fits, preload, flange separation, and assembly limits.
7. Architecture trade: document whether the preferred 1 m, 400 mm, six-stage,
   one-turbine, single-spool candidate is feasible; every change needs signed rationale.

Native solver files may remain with partners, but the repository requires the
neutral `icad.analysis.result.v1` summary plus immutable referenced artifacts.
