# EASA CS-E Amendment 8 development applicability

This matrix is a planning index. The signed applicability determination from the
responsible airworthiness specialist remains authoritative.

| Area | Development treatment | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Configuration and interfaces | Applicable to controlled demonstrator definition | Open |
| Materials and manufacturing methods | Applicable | Open |
| Safety analysis and hazardous effects | Development analogue | Open |
| Fire prevention and flammable-fluid systems | Development analogue | Open |
| Rotor integrity, overspeed and life | Development analogue | Open |
| Vibration surveys and blade/vane response | Development analogue | Open |
| Compressor/turbine blade containment | Development analogue plus test-cell containment | Open |
| Engine control, limiting and instrumentation | Development analogue | Open |
| Fuel, oil, ignition and drainage | Development analogue | Open |
| Endurance and post-test teardown | Development analogue, schedule approved by partner | Open |
| Instructions for operation and maintenance | Applicable to ground demonstrator | Open |
| Icing, ingestion, relight and aircraft installation | Deferred to any future flight program | Deferred |
| Type certificate, production approval, ICA approval | Outside this ground program | Deferred |

The basis must be rechecked at each design freeze. Proposed amendments are not
treated as adopted requirements.
